About

We do one thing, exceptionally well.

Frontpaged is an SEO and Generative Engine Optimization agency working with high-ticket local businesses — medical, legal, and premium service practices — across the United States. We get you found on Google and named by AI assistants when your next customer asks for a recommendation.

We don’t run ads, build brands, or manage your Instagram. There are people who do those things well. We do the thing almost nobody in your industry is doing yet.

How we prove it

We did all of it to ourselves first.

This site is the portfolio. Every technique we sell is deployed on the pages you’re reading — answer-first structure, a full schema graph, a glossary and FAQ hub built for extraction, and a blog publishing on a fixed schedule.

Ask an AI assistant about SEO for your industry and see what it says. That result — good or bad on any given day — is the most honest demonstration we can offer.

How we work

The same four steps for every client, in the same order, because the order is what makes it work.

01

We measure where you actually stand

Before anything else we run your business through ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google with the questions your customers ask, and record who gets named instead of you. That baseline is what every later claim of progress gets measured against.

02

We rebuild the pages that decide whether someone contacts you

One service per page, each answering cost, qualification, process, and what happens next in a structure engines can extract from. Most businesses are invisible for most of what they offer simply because the page doesn't exist.

03

We make your business machine-readable

Schema markup declaring what you are, where you operate, what you offer, and at what price — so an AI system reads facts about your business rather than inferring them from marketing prose.

04

We publish consistently and report honestly

Content every month, because AI visibility rewards sustained signal over bursts. Then a monthly report showing rankings, AI citations, and what moved — including the months when the answer is 'not much yet'.

What we won’t do

Most of what makes an agency trustworthy is the list of things it refuses. Here’s ours.

We never guarantee rankings

Google controls the algorithm; nobody can promise a position. We guarantee the one thing we control instead, and we put it in writing.

One client per market

We won't take two businesses competing for the same searches in the same market. If your market is taken we'll say so on the first call, not after you've signed.

No fake reviews, ever

We build the process for earning genuine reviews and never write or solicit fabricated ones. Beyond the ethics, it's the fastest way to lose a Google Business Profile.

No outcome promises in your content

We won't publish copy promising a specific outcome — a verdict, a sale price, a clinical result. It creates regulatory exposure for you and performs badly in categories Google holds to a stricter standard.

Every AI draft gets a human pass

We use AI to research and draft. Nothing reaches you unedited and unchecked, because unreviewed AI content in a regulated category is a liability, not an efficiency.

Month to month, always

No annual lock-in. If the work isn't earning its place you should be able to stop, and that constraint keeps us honest.

The one thing we do guarantee

We agree up to ten target questions with you in writing in month one — the actual questions your buyers ask. If none of them returns a Frontpaged-produced page or a mention of your business in ChatGPT, Perplexity, or Google AI Overviews within 90 days of the first publish, month four is free.

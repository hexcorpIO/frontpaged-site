Frontpaged collects three kinds of information: what you type into our forms, standard analytics about how the site is used, and, if you arrived from an ad or a campaign link, which campaign that was. We use it to reply to you, to understand which pages and campaigns work, and for nothing else. We do not sell it, rent it or share it with advertisers. To see or delete anything we hold about you, email [hello@frontpaged.io](mailto:hello@frontpaged.io).

This policy covers frontpaged.io and took effect on 27 October 2026. Frontpaged is based in Nashville, Tennessee, and works with clients across the United States.

## What we collect, and why

### Forms you fill in

The contact form and the "send me the plan" form on the AI readiness check ask for your name, email address and business name, and optionally a phone number, your industry and a message. The readiness check also sends your score.

Each submission is stored in our website's database, with the page you submitted it from and your IP address, so that an inquiry can never be lost to a failed email. It is then emailed to us through [Formspree](https://formspree.io/legal/privacy-policy/), a form-delivery service, which keeps a copy under its own privacy policy.

We use what you send to reply to you and, if you become a client, to run the engagement. We keep inquiries for as long as we are talking to you, and for up to two years afterwards so we can pick up a conversation where it left off. Email us to have one deleted sooner.

Please do not include patient, client or case details in a form. It is not a secure channel for that kind of information, and we do not need it to answer you.

### Booking a call

Calls are booked through [Calendly](https://calendly.com/privacy). What you enter there is held by Calendly under its policy and shared with us so we can run the meeting. If you reached the booking page from a campaign, we pass the campaign name along with the booking so we know what brought you (see "Campaign attribution" below).

### Analytics

We use Google Tag Manager and Google Analytics 4 to understand how the site is used: which pages are read, which links and buttons are clicked, how far people get through the readiness check, and whether a visit ends in a form or a booking. Google sets cookies on your browser for this and processes the data under [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). Reports we see are aggregated. We do not use Google Analytics to identify you personally, and we do not use its advertising features.

### Campaign attribution

If you arrive on a link that carries campaign parameters (the `utm_` values marketers add to links, or an ad click identifier such as Google's `gclid`), we store those values in a cookie called `fp_click` for 90 days. It holds only the campaign values, not anything about you. When you book a call we attach those values to the booking so we can tell which campaign produced it. If you arrive without campaign parameters, the cookie is not written.

### Server logs and protection against abuse

Our host, Hostinger, keeps standard web-server logs (IP address, time, page requested, browser) for security and troubleshooting. To slow down automated form abuse we keep a count of submissions per IP address for one hour; the address itself is stored only as a one-way hash.

## Cookies

| Cookie | Set by | Purpose | Lifetime |
|---|---|---|---|
| `_ga`, `_ga_*` | Google Analytics | Tells one visitor's sessions apart in aggregate reports | Up to 2 years |
| `fp_click` | frontpaged.io | Remembers the campaign that brought you here | 90 days |

We do not show a cookie banner. The cookies above run by default, and you can block or clear them in your browser at any time without losing access to anything on the site. The [Google Analytics opt-out add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout) blocks Google's cookies on every site, not just ours.

## Who else sees your data

Only the services named above, each for the purpose stated: our hosting provider, Formspree, Calendly and Google. We do not sell personal information, we do not share it with data brokers or advertisers, and we have not in the past twelve months. We would disclose it if the law required us to.

## Your choices

- **See, correct or delete** what we hold about you: email [hello@frontpaged.io](mailto:hello@frontpaged.io) from the address you used and we will answer within ten business days. You will not be treated differently for asking.
- **Stop emails from us:** reply to any message and say so.
- **Block analytics or attribution cookies:** use your browser settings or the opt-out add-on linked above.

## Children

This site is for business owners and is not directed at anyone under 16. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

## Changes

If this policy changes materially we will update the date at the top and, if you have an open inquiry with us, tell you by email.

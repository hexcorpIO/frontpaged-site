## Method, and its limits

Queries were run through web search and the results inspected for `frontpaged.io`.

**What this does NOT capture:** live ChatGPT and Perplexity responses. Those cannot be
queried programmatically from our tooling, and they are the more important half.
**Someone needs to run the same questions in ChatGPT and Perplexity and screenshot the
answers.** Without that, the "before" is incomplete — see the checklist at the end.

Treat the searches below as a proxy for discoverability, not as a measurement of AI
citation.

## What this tells us

1. **We are not discoverable, including by name.** Expected for content one day old,
   but it is the baseline and it is now recorded.

2. **The competitive set publishes numbers.** AEO Engine states prompt counts, citation
   counts, and a traffic delta. Our strongest current asset is 67 healthcare practices
   of prior SEO/AEO/martech direction — real, but a track record rather than a result.
   The gap closes with the first client, not with more site work.

3. **Comparison listicles are a distribution channel we are absent from.** Several of
   the top results across verticals are "best N agencies" roundups. Getting included is
   a different motion from ranking, and probably a faster one.

4. **"Front Page Agency" is a brand-collision risk worth watching.** Not a reason to
   rename, but it will dilute brand-query results until our own entity is established —
   which is what the `Organization` + `Person` schema and the LinkedIn `sameAs` are for.

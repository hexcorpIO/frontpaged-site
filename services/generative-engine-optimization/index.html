Quick answer

Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) is the discipline of structuring your content and markup so AI-powered search engines — ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews — cite your business by name. That means more high-intent customers discovering you before they ever open a browser tab — nationwide. GEO doesn’t replace traditional SEO — it layers on top of it to capture the next wave of how customers search.

GEO vs. SEO

AI search doesn’t rank pages. It names sources.

Traditional SEO is about ranking: you get your business to position one on a Google results page and let customers click through. It’s still essential — and it’s one of the core things we do.

But AI search works differently. When a customer types “what’s the best option near me?” into ChatGPT or Google’s AI Overview, they don’t get ten blue links. They get a paragraph that names one or two businesses and explains why. The AI synthesizes its answer from indexed content, structured data, and trust signals — then cites the source it found most credible.

GEO is the practice of engineering those signals. Answer-first formatting, FAQ schema, entity consistency, citation building — all of it works together so the AI picks your business when a customer searches in your area. We cover both disciplines because you need both to win in today’s search landscape.

How it works

What makes an AI recommend your business over the one down the street

AI engines don’t flip a coin. They weight specific signals when deciding which business to name. These are the four that move the needle most across every industry we work in — and the ones we build into everything we do.

01

Answer-first content AI can actually quote

AI engines don't browse — they retrieve. When a customer asks ChatGPT for the best provider in their area, it pulls the clearest, most direct answer it can find. We write your pages so that answer is yours: specific, credible, structured to be extracted verbatim.

02

Schema markup that identifies you as an entity

FAQPage, Service, and Organization schema tell AI what your business does, who you serve, and where you operate — in a language the model can read without guessing. Without it, you're just text. With it, you're a named, citable source.

03

Citations and E-E-A-T signals that earn trust

AI engines weight sources that other authoritative sites reference. We build the content strategy — topical depth, consistent NAP, review volume, and external mentions — that positions your business as the credible answer rather than a random result.

04

Consistent, accurate information everywhere it's looked up

ChatGPT and Perplexity cross-reference Google Business Profile, your website, directories, and review platforms. Inconsistent names, addresses, or service lists create confusion that knocks you off the shortlist. We audit and align every signal.

Questions

Generative Engine Optimization — FAQ

What is generative engine optimization (GEO)?+–

Generative engine optimization is the practice of structuring your website content, schema markup, and off-site signals so that AI-powered search engines — ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google's AI Overviews — recommend and cite your business when a user asks a relevant question. Where traditional SEO targets ranked links, GEO targets direct AI citations.

Does GEO replace traditional SEO for my business?+–

No — they reinforce each other. Strong local SEO (ranking on Google, winning the map pack) gives AI engines a credible source to cite. We run both together: the same answer-first content that earns Google rankings also gives AI something concrete to quote. You don't have to choose.

Which AI tools does GEO affect?+–

Our GEO work targets the tools your customers are most likely to use: ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google's AI Overviews. As AI search evolves, the underlying signals — authoritative content, structured data, consistent entity information — transfer across platforms.

How do you measure GEO results?+–

We run regular AI citation tests — prompting ChatGPT, Perplexity, and others with the queries your customers actually ask — and track whether your business is named. We also monitor AI Overview appearances in Google Search Console and report on citation share over time, alongside traditional ranking and traffic data.

How long does it take to start appearing in AI answers?+–

Most businesses begin seeing AI citations within 90 to 120 days of consistent GEO work. Google AI Overviews tend to respond faster than ChatGPT because they pull from indexed content in near real time. The compounding nature of content and schema means results accelerate the longer we work together.
